About

Most engineers pick a lane — backend or frontend, code or design. I never managed to. I care about the schema and the spacing.

That instinct shapes how I work. Since 2023 I've been gathering requirements directly from clients, owning timelines end to end, and shipping products where the engineering and the interface are considered together — Django and Flask on the server, JavaScript up front, Flutter when it needs to travel, and Figma before any of it gets built.
